Smurfettes Cleaning Service is a Perth-based cleaning company built on one simple idea — every home and business deserves to be properly clean. We show up on time, work to a high standard, and leave every space looking and smelling fresh.

Our team is fully trained, police-cleared, and insured. Whether you need a weekly tidy, a thorough end of lease clean, or a full commercial contract, we have the experience and the work ethic to get the job done right the first time.

We service all Perth metropolitan suburbs, and we treat every property — from a studio apartment to a large commercial office — with the same care and attention to detail.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

What cleaning services do you offer in Perth?

We offer residential house cleaning, commercial and office cleaning, end of lease and bond cleaning, deep cleaning, carpet and upholstery cleaning, and window cleaning. All services are available across the Perth metropolitan area.

Do you do end of lease cleaning in Perth?

Yes — end of lease cleaning is one of our most popular services. We follow real estate agent inspection checklists and clean every area thoroughly, including ovens, range hoods, skirting boards, window tracks, and blinds, to give you the best chance of getting your full bond returned.

Are your cleaners police cleared and insured?

Yes. Every member of our team is police-cleared and fully trained before entering any home or business. We carry public liability insurance so you have complete peace of mind throughout every clean.

How do I book a clean or get a quote?

Simply fill out our contact form below, give us a call, or send us an email. Tell us what type of clean you need and the size of your property, and we'll provide a free, no-obligation quote. We respond to all enquiries the same day.

Which suburbs in Perth do you service?

We cover all Perth metro suburbs including the CBD, North Perth, South Perth, East Perth, West Perth, Fremantle, Cottesloe, Subiaco, Mount Lawley, Joondalup, Rockingham, Armadale, Midland, Balcatta, Cannington, and everywhere in between.
