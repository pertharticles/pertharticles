Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

What luxury services does Danao Group provide across Australia?

Danao Group provides a comprehensive suite of elite services including luxury chauffeur transport, private security and close protection, personal concierge and lifestyle management, private jet charter, superyacht charter, and bespoke event curation. All services are available throughout Australia, with international support for Platinum and Black tier members.

How do I apply for Danao Group membership?

Membership is by application or referral only and is not open to the general public. Gold tier members may apply directly or by invitation. Platinum tier requires a referral from an existing member. Black tier is strictly by private invitation. To begin the application process, submit an enquiry through our secure contact form and our team will respond within 24 hours.

Are Danao Group services available 24 hours a day?

Yes. Members have uninterrupted access to concierge support and chauffeur services around the clock, every day of the year. Our operations team monitors all active engagements continuously to ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

How does Danao Group protect client privacy and confidentiality?

Discretion is the foundation of everything we do. All staff operate under comprehensive non-disclosure agreements and strict privacy protocols. Client identities, schedules, engagements, and communications are never disclosed. Our operations are designed so that our involvement remains completely invisible to the outside world.

Which cities in Australia does Danao Group operate in?

Danao Group maintains an active network across all major Australian cities including Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, and the Gold Coast, as well as regional areas. Wherever you need us, we are there.
